# Module: sounds
**Role:** The sound browser (backlog items 017 and 023): lists every sound of the loaded character's `.snd` grouped by group index (collapsible, like the sprite browser), plays one at a time through an injectable `SoundPlayer` (real implementation: Web Audio, one `AudioBuffer` per decoded sound object so a replaced sound never plays its old audio), and has four states: populated, empty (the `.def` references no `.snd`), error (`files.sndIssue`), and per-row "Can't be played". When the caller supplies `editing` (only for a character with a loaded `.snd`), it also imports a WAV as a new sound (group and number fields defaulting to the selected sound's group and the first free number), and for the selected sound replaces its audio or deletes it after a confirm that states how many controllers use it. Pending edits are an ordered log in the document store; the list shows them merged over the parsed sounds. Re-rendered on Undo/Redo: the open groups and the selected sound live per root and survive, playback stops.
**Files:** `src/sounds/sound-browser.ts`, `src/sounds/sound-edits.ts`, `src/sounds/sound-editing.ts`
**Exports:** `renderSoundBrowser(root, character, files, options?)`, `createWebAudioPlayer()`, `SoundPlayer`, `SoundBrowserOptions`, `SoundBrowserEditing`, `SoundBrowserFiles`; `SoundEdit`, `mergeSoundGroups`, `nextFreeSample`, `countSoundReferences`, `SoundReferences`; `prepareSoundEdit`, `buildEditedSnd`, `SoundEditRequest`
**Depends on:** `modules/wasm.md` (`CharacterData.sounds`, `Sound`, the `snd` bridge), `modules/problems.md` (`import.sound`), `modules/i18n.md`, `modules/input.md` (`readFileAsBytes`), `@openkakutou/web-ui-kit`, `modules/problems.md` (`createInlineError`, `createProblemView`), `src/ui/` helpers (`createFilePicker`, `createListRow`), `i18n/plural.ts`

A sound that cannot be played shows a localized row; its raw reason sits behind "Show details". A refused import or replacement shows the `import.sound` problem with the `snd` module's message as its detail. `sound-edits.ts` is pure; `sound-editing.ts` is the async, WASM-backed counterpart. See `.vibe/decisions/021-sound-edits-overlay-replayed-through-snd-wasm.md`.

A sound edit is prepared against the edit log as it stood (`prepareSoundEdit`), and only recorded if the log is still that same one (`main.ts` keeps which log each prepared edit was checked against): an Undo or another edit in the meantime makes `commit` refuse, and the browser says the sounds changed. The browser also refuses a delete while an import or replace is being checked, and a render that was replaced meanwhile records nothing.
