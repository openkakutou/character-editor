# Module: history
**Role:** The shared cross-editor undo/redo history and the unsaved-changes guard (backlog item 010), built on `@openkakutou/web-ui-kit`'s `CommandStack` primitive rather than a bespoke implementation. `app-history.ts` owns one module-level `CommandStack` instance every mutating call in `document`, `palettes`, and `save` pushes onto (`pushHistoryCommand`), plus a separate "dirty" flag — deliberately not derived from `CommandStack.canUndo`, since saving doesn't erase undo history and `canUndo` alone would keep reporting "unsaved" forever after the save that was supposed to clear it (`.vibe/decisions/012`). `unsaved-changes-guard.ts` is the `beforeunload` listener, DOM-free logic (`handleBeforeUnload`) kept separate from the real `window.addEventListener` wiring (`installUnsavedChangesGuard`), installed once at app bootstrap rather than inside `app`'s render function.
**Files:** `src/history/app-history.ts`, `src/history/unsaved-changes-guard.ts`
**Exports:** `getAppHistory(): CommandStack`, `pushHistoryCommand(command: Command): void`, `isDirty(): boolean`, `markClean(): void`, `resetAppHistoryForTests(): void` (app-history.ts); `handleBeforeUnload(event, dirty?): void`, `installUnsavedChangesGuard(target?, dirty?): void`, `UnloadEventLike`, `UnloadEventTarget` (unsaved-changes-guard.ts)
**Depends on:** `@openkakutou/web-ui-kit` (`CommandStack`, `Command`, `CommandStackOptions` — declared in `src/types/web-ui-kit.d.ts`, the package ships no types of its own)
